We aim to handle personal data in accordance with applicable data protection laws, including the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR), the Data Protection Act 2018 and, where applicable, the UAE Federal Personal Data Protection Law.

2. Personal data we collect

The information we collect depends on how you use our website and telephone service.

2.1 Website visitors

We may collect:

  • Technical information in server logs, including your IP address, browser type, device information, date and time of access, and pages requested.
  • Your cookie preferences, including choices stored on your device.
  • Website analytics information, where analytics tools are enabled and you have provided any consent required by law.

2.2 Telephone callers

When you telephone Private Secretary, we may process:

  • The telephone number associated with your call, where available to us.
  • The date, time and duration of the call.
  • Information you tell our adviser when requesting a service.
  • Notes made by our adviser to carry out your instructions.
  • Information required to arrange appointments, bookings, letters, diary tasks or other requested services.
  • Names, dates, contact details and preferences that you ask us to pass to a restaurant, hotel, clinic, travel provider or another organisation.
  • Information reasonably necessary to handle enquiries, complaints, billing disputes and legal matters.

Your telephone network or service provider may separately process call and billing information under its own terms and privacy policy.

Call recording: Calls are not recorded. If recording is introduced, we will update this policy and provide appropriate notice before recording begins.

2.3 Health and other sensitive information

If you ask us to arrange a medical, dental or similar appointment, you may disclose information about your health or other sensitive matters.

Where this constitutes special-category personal data under the UK GDPR, we will process it only where an appropriate lawful basis and an applicable special-category condition are satisfied. Where explicit consent is the applicable condition, we will obtain that consent before processing.

You may withdraw consent where processing relies on consent. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing that took place before withdrawal.

Please provide sensitive information only where it is necessary to complete the service you have requested.

3. How we use your personal data

We may use personal data for the following purposes:

  • Providing the virtual secretary and concierge services you request, including letters, diary tasks, appointments and bookings.
  • Contacting third parties on your behalf when you instruct us to do so.
  • Administering telephone billing arrangements and responding to billing disputes.
  • Managing enquiries, complaints and service issues.
  • Maintaining service records and records reasonably required for regulatory compliance.
  • Operating, maintaining and protecting our website.
  • Analysing website use where permitted and, where required, with your consent.
  • Meeting legal obligations and establishing, exercising or defending legal claims.

We will not use personal data for unrelated purposes without an appropriate lawful basis or other authorisation required by law.

4. Our lawful bases for processing

Depending on the circumstances, we rely on the following lawful bases under applicable data protection law:

Contract and requested services. Processing information necessary to provide the service you request or take steps at your request before entering into a contract.

Legitimate interests. Processing reasonably necessary to operate and secure the website, maintain appropriate service records, deal with enquiries and complaints, prevent misuse, and establish or defend legal claims, provided those interests are not overridden by your rights.

Consent. Processing for optional analytics or for sensitive personal information where consent is the appropriate legal basis and condition.

Legal obligation. Processing required to comply with applicable laws, regulatory requirements, accounting obligations or lawful requests from competent authorities.

Where we rely on legitimate interests, you may have the right to object in accordance with applicable law.

5. Who we share personal data with

We share personal data only where necessary for the purposes described in this policy and where a lawful basis permits us to do so.

Recipients may include:

  • Website hosting and technical support providers.
  • Telephone networks, telecommunications providers and service providers involved in call billing.
  • Restaurants, hotels, travel providers, clinics and other organisations you ask us to contact.
  • Professional advisers and service providers who assist us with legal, accounting, security or operational matters.
  • Relevant regulators, complaint-handling bodies, supervisory authorities or public authorities, where disclosure is required by law or is otherwise lawful and necessary.

Where you ask us to make a booking or contact a third party, we will disclose only information reasonably necessary to carry out your instructions.

Third-party organisations may act as independent data controllers and will handle information under their own privacy notices and legal obligations.

We do not sell personal data.

The specific providers and regulators involved may change as the business develops. We will keep this section accurate and update it where material changes to our processing practices require it.

OCTATEL HUB - FZCO is a Dubai-registered company providing services to UK and international customers.

Personal data may be processed in the United Arab Emirates, the United Kingdom or other countries, depending on the locations of our personnel, hosting infrastructure and service providers.

Where the UK GDPR applies, we will ensure that any restricted transfer of personal data outside the United Kingdom complies with the applicable international transfer requirements. Depending on the circumstances, this may involve an applicable adequacy regulation, the UK International Data Transfer Agreement, the UK Addendum to standard contractual clauses or another permitted transfer mechanism.

Where UAE data protection law applies, we will also comply with the applicable requirements governing international transfers.

You may contact us at hello@privatesecretary.online to request information about relevant transfers and safeguards.

We will identify specific transfer mechanisms only where they have been verified and are actually in place.

7. How long we retain personal data

We retain personal data only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, including legal, accounting, regulatory, security and dispute-resolution requirements.

  • Call notes and booking details: 24 months after the call, unless required for a complaint, dispute or legal claim. Where that applies, the maximum is six years.
  • Server logs: up to 12 months.
  • Cookie preferences: until you clear the relevant site data or change your preference.
  • Analytics information: in accordance with the relevant analytics configuration and retention settings, and only where the required consent has been obtained.

Where information is no longer needed, we will securely delete or anonymise it, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by applicable law.

8. Your data protection rights

Depending on the applicable law and the circumstances, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to your personal data.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Request deletion of your personal data where the legal conditions are met.
  • Request restriction of processing.
  • Object to processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Request data portability where that right applies.
  • Withdraw consent where processing relies on consent.
  • Complain to the relevant data protection supervisory authority.

These rights are subject to applicable legal conditions and exceptions. For example, we may need to retain certain information to comply with a legal obligation or establish or defend a legal claim.

To exercise your rights, email hello@privatesecretary.online.

We may request information reasonably necessary to verify your identity. We will respond within the time limit required by applicable law. Under the UK GDPR, the usual response period is one month, subject to applicable rules on extensions.
