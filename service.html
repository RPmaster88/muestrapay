2. The price

Call cost £3.60 per minute, or part of a minute, plus your phone company’s access charge.

  • Our charge is £3.60 for each minute, or part of a minute, that the call is connected. That includes any time you are waiting to be answered or are on hold. The £3.60 is the full service charge. If we are registered for VAT, VAT is included in it. We do not add a separate VAT amount.
  • Your phone company adds its own access charge. We do not set that charge and it is not included in the £3.60. We cannot tell you the access charge in advance, because it depends on your phone company.
  • There is no subscription and no minimum term. You pay only for the time the call is connected. The contract for that call ends when you hang up.
  • The charge is added to the bill, or the prepay balance, for the telephone number used to call us. If you are not the bill payer, you need that person’s permission before you call. The bill payer is responsible for the cost.
  • Hanging up stops any further per-minute charge. Charges for time already connected still apply.

The same price is shown in the banner at the top of this website and next to the call buttons.

3. Who may call

This is a paid phone service for adults in the UK. Do not call if you are under 18. Do not call if you do not have the bill payer’s permission.

If paying £3.60 per minute would cause you difficulty, or you are unsure what the call will cost, hang up. We will explain the price in plain language if you ask, and we will pause so you can decide. We will not ask you to stay on the line. If you tell us you do not want to continue, we will stop.

4. What the call includes

On the call, an adviser can help with everyday secretarial and concierge tasks you ask for, for example:

  • a diary entry, and a reminder you ask us to send before something is due;
  • a straightforward letter or email, drafted for you to send, and sent to you;
  • talking through a household form so you can complete it yourself;
  • calling a third party on your behalf while you are on the line, or from the notes you give us, and telling you the outcome;
  • asking a restaurant, hotel or travel provider about availability and, if you agree, passing on a booking request and sending you any reference we receive.

There is no standing retainer. A new task, or more time with an adviser, is a new call at the same rate. If we agree on the call to email you a draft, a confirmation, a booking reference or a reminder after you hang up, that message is part of the task from the call. It is not a further charge by itself. We send it only to the address you give us, and only for the task you asked for.

5. What is not included

  • We do not give legal, tax, immigration, benefits or financial advice, and we do not give medical advice. We are not authorised by the Financial Conduct Authority or the Solicitors Regulation Authority. Talking through a form is not advice on whether you qualify.
  • We do not send a letter or message to a third party unless you ask us to do that on the call.
  • The price of a table, room, ticket or fare is charged by that supplier, to you, on the card or account you give them. It is not part of the call cost and we do not take payment for it.
  • A supplier may refuse a booking, apply its own terms, or ask for a deposit. We cannot override that.
  • We do not guarantee that a venue, appointment or journey will be available, or that a third party will answer.

6. Starting and ending the service

The service for a call starts when your call connects. The price is stated on this website before you call. By calling and staying on the line, you ask us to start immediately, and you acknowledge that you lose the 14-day right to cancel time that has already been supplied. You can stop any further supply by hanging up. Hanging up does not undo time already connected.

If we cannot help with your request, we will say so. You can hang up. You will still be charged for the time the call was connected. This does not affect your rights if the service is not performed with reasonable care and skill.

Tell us if a detail is wrong. We rely on what you say on the call. You are responsible for instructions you give us and for information you ask us to pass to a third party. How we use personal data is explained in the Privacy policy.

8. Enquiries, complaints and refunds

Email hello@privatesecretary.online. That route is free. Do not use the paid number to complain or to ask for a refund. Include your name, the number you called from, the date and approximate time of the call, and what went wrong.

We handle an email as follows.

  • We acknowledge it within five working days, starting with the day we receive it.
  • We tell you how it is progressing until it is decided.
  • We send a written decision without undue delay, with the reasons.
  • We keep a written record of the complaint and of the decision.

If you ask for a refund of our per-minute charge, the decision will say whether a refund will be paid, the reasons, the amount, and the date it will be paid. Where we agree a refund, we arrange it as a credit to the next bill for the number that was charged, unless you and we agree another method. We do not charge you for paying the refund. A refund of the access charge is a matter for your phone company.

We will refund our per-minute charge for time when the call failed because of our systems before an adviser could help, or when the charge was applied in error.

If you are dissatisfied with our handling or our decision, you can take the complaint to Ofcom at ofcom.org.uk, or on 0300 123 3333 or 020 7981 3040. Ofcom does not decide individual refunds. Communications Ombudsman and CISAS do not cover this service. You can ask Citizens Advice for guidance, and you can go to court.
